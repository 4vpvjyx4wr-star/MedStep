anatomy, biomechanics, and osteopathic principles of the cervical

naming of cervical somatic dysfunctions. Emphasis is placed on safe
integration of special tests, risk screening, and thoughtful selection of
osteopathic manipulative treatment. The goal is not only recall, but
diagnostic precision and safe clinical judgment.

47. During a C2 to C7 segmental exam, which bony landmark should the
operator contact to most directly localize rotation to a single cervical
segment?
A. Articular pillar
B. Vertebral body
C. Spinous process tip
D. Mastoid process
E. Uncovertebral joint
Answer: A. Articular pillar. The articular pillar (lateral mass) is the
preferred contact point for localizing and introducing rotation and
sidebending at an individual cervical segment. The other landmarks
either are not palpable segment by segment or do not transmit the
force effectively.

49. On a model of a typical cervical vertebra, a student is asked to
identify the structures that form the joints of Luschka and help regulate
cervical motion. Which structures should the student point to?
A. The spinous process
B. The uncinate processes

D. The superior articular facets

Answer: B. The uncinate processes. The joints of Luschka, or
uncovertebral joints, are formed by the uncinate processes along the
superolateral rims of the typical cervical vertebral bodies. These are
important regulators of cervical spinal motion.
